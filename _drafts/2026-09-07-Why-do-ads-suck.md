Ads. You get them, I get them, it's a normal part of everyday life. But over the years, it seems we have been getting more and more annoyed about them, because they seem to be getting more and more annoying. But why? Fundementally, they really aren't *that* different from the T.V. advertisments some have grown a nostalgia for.

Maybe that's it. Maybe it's nostalgia. But that argument falls apart when you see those too young to have that nostalgia wish for those days. Perhaps, it might be from seeing them through the rose-tinted glasses those with that nostalgia wear?

Maybe, but I think it's more than that. 

<!--more-->

Legally speaking, yes actually, T.V. ads are very different. They have different rules, different regulations, and most importantly, different audiences with different technologies. 

### Here's the nerd part:

Television ads can't be shown based off of what they think your interests are, because they don't have the technology for that. They don't know who specifically is watching a *Fast and Furious* marathon on the movie network on a friday that starts at 3:00 P.M.

They have an idea, but they don't know for sure. Even then, that idea they have is more of a generalized one. All they can really think about is, "what kind of person would watch this crap at this specific time?" and make assumptions that theres a good chance it would be people aged roughly from teens to twenty-year-olds, and that those would most likely be males because of the demographic of the movie.

Those assumptions are based off of not only the Movie/T.V. show being aired, but the time it is being aired, because ~3:00 P.M. is usually the time most kids get off school. The **teenagers**. The type of movie playing is also made for people into high octane action scenes and stuff like that. Most people grow out of that, but that usually doesnt happen until after their 20s. The **twenty year olds**. And the general stereotype: females(generally) don't care for that kind of thing. **The males** do. This is their "target demographic".

Based off those assumptions, which are obviously not always accurrate and also generalize a lot of stuff, they will air certain types of ads specifically catering to their target demographic.
