Where are you?

Seriously. How did you get here? Where did you come from? Did you even realize what you were doing when you were sent here? You see, that's a big problem nowadays, is people never come to places like this on their own accord. They don't type random urls or google search random stuff, and even when they do, all those searches show them are the biggest websites owned by the largest corporations. Random little websites like this, for the most part, are dead. And when they're not dead, they certainly don't feel alive either...

What do I mean?

Most of the time, even when you are visiting these sites, there usually has to be some specific reason for it. Most people don't come onto random websites like this for fun. That used to be the majority, but times shift. I mean, why would they even come here to watch like, 20 memes and nothing else, when they can watch infinitely more memes on YouTube? Why would you come here to learn about something that has an objectively better and more comprehensive explaination on wikipedia? hell, why would you use wikipedia when you can get an ai to do all that source tracking for you, and it can give you a simple, easy-to-digest summary of it? People don't care about people as much as they used to, it seems, and personally, I think a part of it is the fact that, for a very large chunk of people out there, a good ~90% of their interactions with the world, and other people, are by reading text on a screen. This leads to less human connection, less empathy for others. This is just my personal opinion, though, and not 100% of the world is like this. As a matter of fact, I'm willing to say its mainly the larger, more populated countries that suffer from this the most. I don't mean to "fear-monger", but I am, I guess you'd call it, a skeptic. I question everything, and whether or not it has a deeper meaning than what's on the surface. What I hope seperates me, from just some dumb conspiracy theorist, is that I understand not everything has that iceberg of secrets or some deeper reasoning behind it. Not everything is done to hurt you, but that doesn't mean everything is done to help you. The only thing that can ever be confirmed, is that something must've caused that thing to happen. All actions have a reaction, and those actions can chain together. If some big tech company releases an update for a device, for example, it's not usually with bad intentions against you, but that doesn't mean that's never a possibility. It's always good to give everything some "deeper thought", but sometimes, you can give too much deeper thought into something that only needed a little. And sometimes, you don't give enough. And thats why you have to think about things, for yourself. Don't sit there and take in other's opinions, form your own, and then consider those outside opinions. Yes, listening to others opinions is good, but not when you only think about those opinions, and end up forgetting your own, eventually, never even having one in the first place. This leads to fear of making your own opinion, because others may judge you for it, or nobody would care about it. But why does it matter what others think about your opinion, it's yours, the the only thing that matters, is that it matters to you.

Just remember to think about things, to have your own opinions, and not to be afraid, of being judged for them. -Yeet
